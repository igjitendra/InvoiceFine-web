Quick Setup Instructions

4 Supported Connection Modes

Configure your printer in less than 30 seconds inside Settings > Printer Settings in the InvoiceFine app.

1. Bluetooth (Direct SPP / BLE)

1. Turn on your printer and open phone Bluetooth settings.
2. Pair the device (default PIN is usually 0000 or 1234).
3. Open InvoiceFine > Settings > Printer Settings > Select your printer name.
4. Tap "Print Test Slip" — you're ready to bill!

2. Wi-Fi / Local Network LAN (Port 9100)

1. Plug your countertop printer to your shop router using an Ethernet cable.
2. Hold the paper FEED button while turning power on to print the printer's IP address.
3. In InvoiceFine > Select Network LAN mode.
4. Enter the printer IP (e.g. 192.168.1.100) and Port 9100.

3. USB Direct via OTG Cable

1. Connect your Android phone or tablet to the printer using a standard USB-C to USB-B OTG adapter.
2. Accept the USB connection permission prompt on your Android screen.
3. Instant zero-configuration printing with zero wireless interference.

4. Android System Print Spooler

1. Use Android's native print framework (Mopria Print Service, HP Print Service, or Epson Print Enabler).
2. Supports standard laser, inkjet, or wireless network printers on the same Wi-Fi.
3. Prints full-size A4 invoices as well as thermal slips.

Buyer's Rule of Thumb Checklist

When purchasing any new thermal printer for your shop, ensure the product description specifies:

  • Command Set: Must support standard ESC/POS command emulation.
  • Paper Width: 58mm (2-inch) for compact portable use, or 80mm (3-inch) for large counter bills.
  • Interface: Bluetooth (for phone/tablet counter sales) or Ethernet/LAN (for multi-device sharing).
