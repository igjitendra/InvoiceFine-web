Mastering Customer Udhar Khata & WhatsApp Reminders

Published: August 2026 • 4 Min Read • By InvoiceFine Accounting Team

The Hidden Cost of Paper Khata Bahi-Khata

In almost every traditional Indian retail and wholesale business, granting credit (udhar) to regular neighborhood customers is unavoidable. However, using traditional paper registers leads to:

  • Misplaced pages and unrecorded partial payments.
  • Awkward customer disputes over past dates or forgotten items.
  • Lack of a single view showing total money stuck in the market.

3 Habits of Fast-Collecting Businesses

  1. Link Every Udhar to an Auditable Bill: Never write a loose amount without an invoice snapshot. InvoiceFine ties every credit entry to an exact invoice number.
  2. Send Immediate Digital Receipts: As soon as an invoice is finalized with partial payment, share the PDF receipt on WhatsApp. Both buyer and seller have instant written proof.
  3. Polite Weekly WhatsApp Alerts: Customers frequently delay payments simply because they forget. A polite 1-tap WhatsApp reminder containing the exact pending balance, bill number, and your UPI QR code makes repayment instantaneous.

Exporting Formal Ledger Statements

When settling monthly accounts with major wholesale parties or contractors, export InvoiceFine's detailed Date-Wise Ledger Statement PDF showing every debit, credit, and running balance.
