Every Module Built for Maximum Speed

1. Express POS & Rapid Counter Checkout

3-Tap Billing • Camera Barcode Scanner • Dual Grid/List • Quick Cash & Dynamic UPI QR

Engineered specifically for rush-hour counter sales where every second counts. Express POS transforms your Android mobile phone into a high-throughput point-of-sale billing terminal.

  • Dual POS Interface: Seamlessly toggle between a visual 2-column product card grid and a compact 1-column list with +/- quantity micro-steppers for lightning-fast cart adjustments.
  • 1-Tap Camera Barcode Scanner: Integrated right beside the item search bar. Instantly scans barcodes using your device camera without expensive laser barcode guns.
  • Multi-Tender Quick Cash Shortcuts: Pre-calculated cash tender buttons (Exact, ₹100, ₹200, ₹500, ₹1000) that automatically calculate the exact change return owed to the customer.
  • Dynamic On-Screen UPI QR: Generates a live UPI QR code containing the exact bill amount for instant contactless customer payments via Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm.
  • Inline Customer Add: Quickly capture customer name and mobile number without leaving the active billing counter screen.
  • Goods & Services Counter Billing: Switch tabs seamlessly to bill both physical inventory items and service charges on the same counter receipt.

2. Bluetooth, Wi-Fi & USB Thermal Printing

58mm (2 Inch) & 80mm (3 Inch) Support • Sub-Second Print • WhatsApp Digital Slips

Eliminate expensive computer systems and ink refills. InvoiceFine natively connects to any standard ESC/POS thermal printer to generate crisp physical receipts in less than one second.

  • Multi-Protocol Connectivity: Connect via Bluetooth, Local Wi-Fi (Raw Port 9100), or direct USB OTG cable.
  • Standard 58mm & 80mm Paper: Auto-formats receipts for 2-inch and 3-inch paper rolls with customizable shop name, address, GSTIN, phone, and footer thank-you note.
  • Itemized Receipt Layout: Neatly displays product names, quantities, unit prices, discounts, GST breakdown, payment method, and QR code.
  • 1-Tap WhatsApp Digital Receipts: For customers who prefer paperless bills, send a clean, formatted digital receipt directly to their WhatsApp number in one click.

3. Smart GST & Non-GST Invoicing

9 PDF Themes • Rubber Stamps • Quotations • Auto Round-Off

Whether you run a GST-registered business or a non-GST retail shop, InvoiceFine produces professional, tax-compliant documentation with zero calculation errors.

  • Comprehensive GST Calculation: Intelligent intra-state (CGST + SGST) vs inter-state (IGST) tax calculation based on customer state code.
  • 9 Professional PDF Templates: Modern, Classic, Minimal, Elegant, Compact GST, and thermal receipt layouts with your company logo, signature, and terms.
  • Digital Rubber Stamps: Apply authenticated stamps (PAID, PARTIAL, DUE, ESTIMATE, CANCELLED) to PDFs automatically.
  • Document Lifecycle: Save drafts, create quotations/estimates, and convert them to finalized tax invoices with one tap.
  • Automated Mathematical Round-Off: Prevents odd-paise cash confusion at checkout by calculating accurate mathematical round-offs.

4. Official CBIC HSN & SAC Directory

10,000+ Offline Codes • 1-Tap Tax Slab Assignment • 100% Compliant

Never guess GST tax rates or HSN codes again. InvoiceFine includes the complete official Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC) directory stored directly on your phone.

  • 10,000+ Preloaded Codes: Full database of official goods (HSN) and services (SAC) codes available 100% offline.
  • Instant Smart Search: Search by product name or code numbers to quickly pick the right tax category.
  • Automated Tax Slab Mapping: Picking an HSN/SAC code automatically configures the appropriate GST slab (0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, or 28%).
  • Tax Invoice Compliance: Official HSN/SAC codes are clearly shown on all printable thermal receipts and customer PDF invoices.

5. Live Business Performance & Net Profit

Real-Time Margin Tracking • Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) • Day/Month Snapshots

High turnover doesn't mean high profits if expenses and inventory costs aren't tracked. InvoiceFine calculates your true net profit in real time right on your Dashboard.

  • Deterministic Net Profit Formula: Net Profit = Total Recognized Sales − Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) − Shop Expenses.
  • Today vs This Month Toggle: Switch between daily performance and monthly milestones with a single tap.
  • Cash Inflow vs Receivables: Compare actual cash received against pending udhar balances to avoid liquidity crunches.
  • Top Products Analytics: View your fast-moving items, highest revenue generators, and top profitable products.

6. Customer Ledger & Udhar Khata

Zero-Leakage Credit Management • WhatsApp Reminders • FIFO Settlements

Stop losing money to unrecorded customer credit in paper khata books. InvoiceFine maintains an automatic, auditable digital ledger for every single customer.

  • Real-Time Balance Tracking: View individual customer balances and total outstanding receivables across your store directly from the dashboard.
  • 1-Click WhatsApp Reminders: Generate professional, polite reminder messages complete with bill summaries and UPI payment links.
  • FIFO Auto-Clearance: When a customer makes a partial payment, the app automatically settles oldest unpaid invoices first.
  • Customer Account Statement PDFs: Export detailed date-wise account statements to share with parties for transparent settlements.

7. Multi-Unit Inventory & Stock Control

Auto Deduction • Low Stock Warnings • Restock Shortcuts • Scaled Quantity Math

Maintain full control over your stock levels with automatic inventory decrement upon checkout, restock movement history, and low-inventory alerts.

  • Low Stock Badges with "Tap to Restock": Visual alerts appear on dashboard and catalog items when stock drops below threshold. Tap to quickly add new stock.
  • Scaled Quantity Precision: Handles fractional units with exact scaled arithmetic (e.g. 1.250 kg, 0.750 liters, 2.5 meters, pieces, boxes).
  • Wholesale vs Retail Pricing: Configure dual price tiers for B2B bulk buyers and B2C walk-in customers.
  • Stock Movement Audit Trail: Complete history tracking stock additions (purchases), subtractions (sales), manual adjustments, and customer returns.

8. Purchase & Vendor Management

Supplier Directory • Procurement Bills • Auto Stock Intake • Vendor Khata

Organize your upstream supply chain. Track purchase invoices, monitor vendor payment obligations, and automatically replenish catalog stock.

  • Supplier Directory: Record vendor business name, GSTIN, phone number, and payment terms.
  • Automatic Stock Increment: Entering a purchase bill automatically increments product stock counts without manual counting.
  • Supplier Khata & Payments-Out: Keep clear records of payments made to suppliers and track pending liabilities.
  • Purchase History & Bills: Store supplier invoice numbers, dates, and tax breakdowns for complete purchase reconciliation.

9. Daybook & Cash Register Closing

Opening Float • Cash In/Out • Discrepancy Detection • Daily Cashflow PDF

Prevent counter cash pilferage and cashier errors with daily cash register balancing. Track exact physical cash collected against expected sales.

  • Daily Opening Float: Record starting cash in the drawer at shop opening.
  • Automated Inflow & Outflow: Cash sales add to drawer balance; petty cash expenses and vendor payments deduct automatically.
  • Discrepancy Detection: Enter counted cash at closing; InvoiceFine flags any cash shortage or excess immediately.
  • Daily Register Report: Export and print a clean 1-page daily cash summary for owner audits and records.

10. 100% Offline SQLite, Biometrics & CA Reports

Local Encryption • PIN/Fingerprint App Lock • Google Drive Backups • GSTR-1

Gain complete sovereignty over your business data. InvoiceFine never forces you onto remote cloud servers, keeping your trade secrets 100% safe.

  • Local SQLite Database: All tables, customers, inventory, and sales records reside encrypted on your physical Android device.
  • App Lock (PIN & Fingerprint): Secure your billing records and profit numbers from unauthorized staff or device access.
  • 1-Tap Dual Backups: Create encrypted backup files to phone storage or your personal Google Drive with automated 1-click restore.
  • CA-Ready GSTR-1 Export: Export B2B, B2CS, and B2CL tax summaries in clean Excel and CSV formats ready for your tax consultant.
