Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

At InvoiceFine, we respect your privacy and are committed to maintaining the confidentiality of your commercial records. This Privacy Policy outlines how our Android mobile application and website handle information.

1. Device-First & Offline Architecture

InvoiceFine is developed with an offline-first architecture. Your business profile, customer details, product inventories, and generated invoices or estimates are stored locally on your mobile device.

2. Information Collection & Use

The mobile application does not require you to create a proprietary remote cloud account to perform everyday billing operations.

  • Commercial Records: Invoices, prices, tax calculations, and contact directories remain on your device.
  • Camera Access: Camera permissions are used strictly in real-time when you choose to scan product barcodes or QR codes. Camera data is not recorded or transmitted.
  • Storage & Files: Storage permissions are requested solely to allow you to save and export PDF invoices, or to execute manual device backups.

3. Subscription & Purchases

InvoiceFine Pro in-app subscriptions are processed securely via Google Play In-App Billing. We do not process, store, or receive your credit card or sensitive payment details. Google Play manages payment processing according to Google’s standard privacy terms.

4. Data Backup and Export

You retain full control over your backup files. Backup records created within the application are managed by you on your local hardware or your preferred file destination.

5. Third-Party Services

The website and mobile application do not sell your personal or business data to third-party advertising networks.
