Interactive Interface Tour

Designed for Speed & Sunlight Contrast

Explore how InvoiceFine combines Material 3 principles, high contrast visibility in bright shop environments, and 1-tap billing actions.

Screen 01

Welcome & 60-Second Setup

Get straight to business. No cloud accounts, email verifications, or passwords needed. Simply enter your shop name and start creating bills immediately.

  • Zero Friction: Instant offline database provisioning.
  • GST / Non-GST Toggle: Setup your business regime in 1 step.
  • Custom Logo: Add your shop header for professional branding.
Screen 02

⚡ Express POS & Camera Barcode Billing

Built specifically so shopkeepers can complete counter checkout in under 10 seconds. Switch between a 2-column grid and a streamlined list view with +/- micro-steppers.

  • 1-Tap Camera Barcode Scan: Instant item detection directly beside search.
  • Multi-Tender Cash Shortcuts: Exact, ₹100, ₹200, ₹500, ₹1000 with auto change calc.
  • On-Screen Dynamic UPI QR: Contactless instant payment scanning.
  • Inline Customer Add: Save new customer details without leaving billing screen.
Screen 03

🖨️ Bluetooth, Wi-Fi & USB Thermal Printing

Hand physical printed receipts to walk-in customers instantly (<1 sec) on standard 58mm or 80mm rolls without toner ink or bulky computers.

  • 58mm & 80mm Support: Automatic margin and font scaling for rolls.
  • Bluetooth, Wi-Fi & USB: Seamless pairing with standard ESC/POS printers.
  • Receipt Customization: Shop header, GSTIN, itemized list & thank-you footer.
  • 1-Tap WhatsApp Slips: Instant digital receipt sharing for paperless shoppers.
Screen 04

👤 Customer Khata & WhatsApp Reminders

Maintain accurate digital ledgers for all your regular parties. Never lose track of pending customer udhar.

  • Clear Balance Indicators: Real-time dues with FIFO auto debt settlement.
  • 1-Tap WhatsApp Alerts: Polite payment reminders with dynamic UPI links.
  • Statement PDF Export: Full date-wise account statements for transparent settlement.
Screen 05

📦 Inventory & 10,000+ CBIC HSN Directory

Automatic stock deduction upon billing, low inventory threshold notifications with restock shortcuts, and official CBIC HSN/SAC catalog lookup.

  • 10,000+ CBIC Codes: Search official tax codes offline with auto tax slabs (0%-28%).
  • Low Stock Alerts: Visual warning badges on dashboard with "Tap to Restock".
  • Wholesale vs Retail Pricing: Dual price tiers for bulk & walk-in buyers.
  • Scaled Quantity Math: Decimal precision for kg, grams, meters, liters & units.
Screen 06

📊 Live Net Profit, Daybook & GSTR-1

Get immediate financial clarity: Net Profit = Recognized Sales − COGS − Shop Expenses. Daily cash drawer closing with shortage detection.

  • Live Profit Snapshot: "Today" vs "This Month" performance toggle.
  • Daybook Cash Register: Opening float, sales, payments-out & drawer balance.
  • GSTR-1 CA Reports: Ready for your tax consultant in clean Excel/CSV.
