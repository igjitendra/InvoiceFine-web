Interactive Interface Tour

Designed for Speed & Sunlight Contrast

Explore how InvoiceFine combines Material 3 principles, high contrast visibility in bright shop environments, and 1-tap billing actions.

Screen 01

Welcome & 60-Second Setup

Get straight to business. No cloud accounts, email verifications, or passwords needed. Simply enter your shop name and start creating bills immediately.

  • Zero Friction: Instant offline database provisioning.
  • GST / Non-GST Toggle: Setup your business regime in 1 step.
  • Custom Logo: Add your shop header for professional branding.
Screen 02

High-Speed Billing Engine

Built specifically so shopkeepers can complete customer transactions in under 10 seconds. Duplicate taps increment quantity automatically, and customer history pre-fills addresses.

  • Fast Item Search: By SKU, barcode, or product name.
  • Multi-Payment Splits: Combine Cash, UPI, and Udhar.
  • Instant Print: A4 PDFs or 3-inch thermal bills.
Screen 03

Bluetooth Thermal Printing

Hand physical printed receipts to walk-in customers instantly without requiring toner ink or bulky computers.

  • 58mm & 80mm Support: Automatic margin and font scaling.
  • ESC/POS Standard: Works with all standard Bluetooth printers.
  • Receipt Customization: Shop address, GSTIN, and thank you notes.
Screen 04

Customer Khata & Payment Reminders

Maintain accurate digital ledgers for all your regular parties. Never forget pending customer udhar.

  • Clear Balance Indicators: Red for due, green for settled.
  • 1-Tap WhatsApp Alerts: Polite payment reminders with UPI QR.
  • Statement PDF Export: Dispute-free account settlements.
Screen 05

Inventory & Stock Management

Automatic stock deduction upon billing, low inventory threshold notifications, and HSN/SAC catalog management.

  • Low Stock Alerts: Prevent running out of essential items.
  • Wholesale vs Retail Pricing: Dual price tiers.
  • Decimal Quantities: Kilograms, liters, meters, boxes, and units.
Screen 06

P&L & GSTR Tax Analytics

Get immediate financial clarity: Net Profit = Recognized Sales − COGS − Operating Expenses.

  • GSTR-1 Excel/PDF: Ready for your CA in 1 click.
  • Top Products & Customers: Track highest sales drivers.
  • Date Range Filtering: Today, this month, or custom fiscal quarters.
