Refund Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Thank you for choosing InvoiceFine. We strive to provide a seamless business management experience on Android.

1. In-App Purchases via Google Play

All subscriptions and purchases for InvoiceFine Pro are processed through Google Play. Consequently, billing transactions, payment authorizations, and refund requests are governed by Google Play’s official refund policies.

2. How to Request a Refund

If you wish to request a refund for an in-app subscription:

  • Visit the Google Play website or open the Google Play Store app on your Android device.
  • Go to Account > Order History and locate your InvoiceFine purchase.
  • Select Request a refund or report a problem following Google Play's standard refund steps.

3. Canceling Subscriptions

You can cancel your InvoiceFine Pro subscription at any time prior to renewal via the Subscriptions section in Google Play. Cancellation prevents future billing cycles while retaining Pro access until the conclusion of the active billing period.

4. Support Assistance

If you have trouble accessing your Pro entitlement after a purchase or need technical assistance, please reach out to our team with your Google Play order receipt:
Email: support@invoicefine.com
