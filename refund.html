Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Google Play Billing Purchases

All InvoiceFine Pro premium plans and subscriptions are purchased and billed directly through Google Play In-App Billing. Consequently, all payment processing, currency conversion, invoice receipts, and refund requests are governed by Google Play's standard consumer refund policies.

2. How to Request a Refund via Google Play

If you purchased an InvoiceFine Pro subscription by mistake or encountered billing issues:

  1. Open the Google Play Store app or visit play.google.com.
  2. Tap your Profile Icon in the top right > Payments & Subscriptions > Budget & Order History.
  3. Find your InvoiceFine Pro order and select Report a problem or Request a refund.
  4. Google Play typically evaluates and processes refund requests submitted within 48 hours of purchase.

3. How to Cancel Auto-Renewal

You can cancel your subscription renewal at any point without penalty:

  1. Open Google Play Store > Subscriptions.
  2. Select InvoiceFine Pro.
  3. Tap Cancel Subscription.
  4. You will continue to have full Pro access until the end of your current paid billing period.
